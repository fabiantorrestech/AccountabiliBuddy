# AccountabiliBuddy hard rules (full text)

Moved verbatim from the project CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-26; CLAUDE.md keeps a short version. This file is the binding detail: when a feature adds or changes a rule, stored document or synced field, update it here.

> **ADR 45 (2026-10-27): the Wi-Fi link (ADR 14) is deleted.** There is no listening port, pairing, `devices.settings`/`devices.paired`/`devices.identity`/`devices.publicNetworksTold` document (deleted once on upgrade), phone link client or watch PC route (`PcLink`) any more. Every mention below of the ADR 14 link, paired devices over it, the "old way" switch or the watch reaching the PC over Wi-Fi is history. Devices share only through the sealed sync files (ADR 22, 26, 42; the self-hosted relay, ADR 46, is planned), and the watch reaches a PC only through the phone. A PC session with no set end counts as running on the phone and the watch only while its PC file is under 30 minutes old.

> **ADR 46 (2026-10-27): the self-hosted sealed relay.** A new outbound connection: the PC (`CalendarNetwork`, the one auditable class) and the phone (`RelayNetwork`, HttpsURLConnection) talk HTTPS only to the relay address the user enters or joins by QR (`r` address, `p` certificate pin, `k` admission key in the join link). Pinned: fail closed on mismatch, any hostname; unpinned: normal CA validation. The relay stores only the sealed sync files (AES-256-GCM, unchanged format) and can't read them; it sees opaque file names, sizes, times and client addresses. The relay group id and bearer token are derived from the group code with HKDF per relay (bound to its pin or address), never stored in plain text; creating a group on a relay needs its admission key. Devices → Own relay (`sync.settings`: RelayOn, RelayUrl, RelayPin; off until set). "Run a relay on this PC" (`sync.settings`: HostRelayOn, HostRelayPort 8743, HostRelayPublic): the app starts the bundled relay as a job-object child (data in `%LocalAppData%\AccountabiliBuddy\relay`), stops it on quit, adds a Private+Public firewall rule after one elevation prompt, and warns on a public network. Joining a group from a link or a typed code asks twice before leaving the current group.

> **ADR 47 (2026-10-27): day plan modes.** Every guard (work-hours guard, Pre-Work, lights out, the work nudge, comms windows, Cold Turkey key holds, poll speed, the day bar) reads guarded windows only from `DayWorkWindows`: Schedule = work parts or a work window (lunch and rest parts inside it are subtracted); Budget = wake to bedtime (the plan's night, else the sleep settings' night) minus non-productive windows and lunch, guarded until that day's counted focus (never hand-added, test, side-task, planning or away time) reaches the budget, with a pace line; Free = no guard (sleep and alarms unchanged). A plan change that lowers today's guarded work (time or budget hours left: Free, a lower budget, longer breaks, re-mapping today, switching or moving week B, including "This week is A/B") waits as ADR 28 says; more work applies at once. The start-of-day assistant (document "startOfDay", this PC only, never synced; off with the work nudge's "Steer me to start" or its own switch) comes once per workday on the first arrival in or before the first guarded window, as a front card: today's hours (sets today's goal and, on a Budget day, today's budget as a one-off day; raising applies at once, lowering waits or is refused like the goal card's), up to 3 first tasks (Pick if more), then Start (remembered length, planning first); "Not now" needs a reason and asks again in 15 minutes; never on a Free day.

- **Local only, except read-only calendars (ADR 9, supersedes ADR 2), sealed sync files between the user's own devices (ADR 22, 26, 42; the ADR 14 link is deleted, ADR 45) and the watch's snapshot through the phone (ADR 21).** OCR text, window titles, full URLs and reasons never leave the device; The Pixel Watch companion (ADR 21, `android/wear`) gets only a snapshot (today's plan parts, focus session times and profile names, drift times, focused/planned minutes for today and the week, the running session with its current task, that task's number in the list (C 14) and task count (ADR 24), today's lunch (whether it's a workday, the lunch length, start and end), the daily goal in minutes (ADR 29: also in "focusDay"; the phone shows focus against it, the watch an outer ring on the session page and a buzz when it's reached); never window titles, addresses, reasons or notes; plus, ADR 36, the phone's noise player state (playing, the sound's shown name, volume, locked: the headphone lock is on and no headphones) on a Noise page whose buttons and crown (5% a step) send play/pause, skip and volume back to the phone (ADR 42, W 1: also to a PC's noise player through the phone, the page's device switcher going This phone → each PC by name, showing that PC's noise state from its sync file a few seconds behind), the only things the watch can control besides its Lunch page (start, I'm back, +5/−5 through the phone) and its own local timers), and nothing until it's linked on the phone (Settings → Pixel Watch): from the phone over Google's Wearable Data Layer as messages only (Bluetooth when near; otherwise Google's end-to-end encrypted relay, on by default; with the relay off, only while the watch is nearby), or, when the phone can't be reached, from the PC over the ADR 14 link as a paired device of kind "watch" (the state without the profile list and "focusDay", never "settings"; it can't start, end or change anything; paired from the PC's QR code through the phone, "Pair my watch instead"). Each route is a switch on the phone. A watch asking for news makes the phone connect to the PC for a moment, only when an answer can reach the watch. Device sync (ADR 22): progress (the last 14 days of counted sessions and drift, only the fields ADR 15 allows), the day plan, profiles, sleep and day bar settings (and a phone's next alarm, ADR 23, and its estimated wake time per kind of morning, ADR 40: only the two times, today's flexible lunch (start and end; every device takes the newest edit for the day), and a phone's pickups during the other devices' sessions: unlock counts and the longest stretch down, from Usage access, ADR 26; and a phone's smart bedtime, ADR 34: per kind of night (Workdays, Off-Days) only the learned time in bed before waking and its status numbers (nights, time to fall asleep, average time asleep and sleep, made it this week), never sleep sessions or times of phone use) leave a device only as sealed files (AES-256-GCM, key by HKDF from a 40-character link code kept under DPAPI on the PC and the Android Keystore on the phone, never carried with the files), each device writing one file of its own; they're carried by QR codes the PC shows (with an end-of-work reminder), files the user shares or imports, a cloud folder the user picks (on a PC a plain folder, e.g. inside Google Drive for desktop; file names are opaque ids) or the ADR 14 link (the phone carries the other devices' files home, never back the way they came); every method is a switch per device, off until turned on; on a work computer the app opens no connection for it (it only reads and writes the folder; Drive for desktop uploads); a receiver applies what arrives by its own rules (stricter at once, looser after its delay and never during a session, a day plan clash asks); a device with Activity history off sends no sessions or drift; each device's Cold Turkey expected blocks never sync. Any other connection needs a new ADR.
- **Location for the sunset theme (ADR 49).** The PC's location (from Windows location at coarse accuracy, a typed latitude/longitude, or a city from the bundled GeoNames list) is used only to work out sunrise and sunset on the device. It is read from Windows at most once a day and only while the light/dark mode is After sunset, stored DPAPI-encrypted in the `documents` table, never synced, logged or sent anywhere. Turning After sunset off stops reading it; the stored value can be cleared in Settings → Appearance. The theme choice itself (the `theme` document; city and lat/long sit inside its DPAPI-encrypted place payload) is per device and not synced.

- History icons: app icons are read from the program file of a running app; site icons come only from the browser extension (the browser's own favicon cache, Chromium `favicon` permission / Firefox data: URLs). Both are cached as files in the data folder's `icons\`. Never download an icon.
- Detection cascade, cheapest first: process/title/URL rules → OCR only when still unknown. Event-driven, plus a slow periodic recheck. Keep CPU and battery use low.
- Activity history (ADR 8): only times, app (process name), site (domain), the site's section (ADR 17, Reddit, YouTube and GitHub only: `r/<subreddit>`, the channel the extension reports, `owner/repo`; `SiteSections`; adding a site needs the user's OK), classification and session context are stored readable.
