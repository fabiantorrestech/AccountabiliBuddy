# AccountabiliBuddy hard rules (full text)

Moved verbatim from the project CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-26; CLAUDE.md keeps a short version. This file is the binding detail: when a feature adds or changes a rule, stored document or synced field, update it here.

> **ADR 45 (2026-10-27): the Wi-Fi link (ADR 14) is deleted.** There is no listening port, pairing, `devices.settings`/`devices.paired`/`devices.identity`/`devices.publicNetworksTold` document (deleted once on upgrade), phone link client or watch PC route (`PcLink`) any more. Every mention below of the ADR 14 link, paired devices over it, the "old way" switch or the watch reaching the PC over Wi-Fi is history. Devices share only through the sealed sync files (ADR 22, 26, 42; the self-hosted relay, ADR 46, is planned), and the watch reaches a PC only through the phone. A PC session with no set end counts as running on the phone and the watch only while its PC file is under 30 minutes old.

> **ADR 46 (2026-10-27): the self-hosted sealed relay.** A new outbound connection: the PC (`CalendarNetwork`, the one auditable class) and the phone (`RelayNetwork`, HttpsURLConnection) talk HTTPS only to the relay address the user enters or joins by QR (`r` address, `p` certificate pin, `k` admission key in the join link). Pinned: fail closed on mismatch, any hostname; unpinned: normal CA validation. The relay stores only the sealed sync files (AES-256-GCM, unchanged format) and can't read them; it sees opaque file names, sizes, times and client addresses. The relay group id and bearer token are derived from the group code with HKDF per relay (bound to its pin or address), never stored in plain text; creating a group on a relay needs its admission key. Devices → Own relay (`sync.settings`: RelayOn, RelayUrl, RelayPin; off until set). "Run a relay on this PC" (`sync.settings`: HostRelayOn, HostRelayPort 8743, HostRelayPublic): the app starts the bundled relay as a job-object child (data in `%LocalAppData%\AccountabiliBuddy\relay`), stops it on quit, adds a Private+Public firewall rule after one elevation prompt, and warns on a public network. Joining a group from a link or a typed code asks twice before leaving the current group.

> **ADR 47 (2026-10-27): day plan modes.** Every guard (work-hours guard, Pre-Work, lights out, the work nudge, comms windows, Cold Turkey key holds, poll speed, the day bar) reads guarded windows only from `DayWorkWindows`: Schedule = work parts or a work window (lunch and rest parts inside it are subtracted); Budget = wake to bedtime (the plan's night, else the sleep settings' night) minus non-productive windows and lunch, guarded until that day's counted focus (never hand-added, test, side-task, planning or away time) reaches the budget, with a pace line; Free = no guard (sleep and alarms unchanged). A plan change that lowers today's guarded work (time or budget hours left: Free, a lower budget, longer breaks, re-mapping today, switching or moving week B, including "This week is A/B") waits as ADR 28 says, and so does one that makes what is left of today less strict (ADR 63); more work applies at once. The start-of-day assistant (document "startOfDay", this PC only, never synced; off with the work nudge's "Steer me to start" or its own switch) comes once per workday on the first arrival in or before the first guarded window, as a front card: today's hours (sets today's goal and, on a Budget day, today's budget as a one-off day; raising applies at once, lowering waits or is refused like the goal card's), up to 3 first tasks (Pick if more), then Start (remembered length, planning first); "Not now" needs a reason and asks again in 15 minutes; never on a Free day.

ADR 58: the PC's sealed sync file lists calendar events (ADR 26; visible calendars only, yesterday to 14 days ahead) and, on an event that resolves to a day plan category through the classifications and rules in force (never a pending change; "Don't classify" and no match add nothing), also its `categoryId` (a category of the shared day plan), `knownSince` (when this PC first saw the event at its current time and category, by `TrustedClock`; left out while not stamped, which a reader treats as not noticed), `seriesId` and `occurrenceKey` (when the event has them); the written time is the device card's `writtenAt`. An entry may carry `colorEditedAt`. Older apps ignore all of it. It is never in a QR file or a backup, and a file without it leaves the receiver's stored list alone. Category names are the user's text: sealed in transit, never logged. - **Location for the sunset theme (ADR 49).** The PC's location (from Windows location at coarse accuracy, a typed latitude/longitude, or a city from the bundled GeoNames list) is used only to work out sunrise and sunset on the device. It is read from Windows at most once a day and only while the light/dark mode is After sunset, stored DPAPI-encrypted in the `documents` table, never synced, logged or sent anywhere. Turning After sunset off stops reading it; the stored value can be cleared in Settings → Appearance. The theme choice itself (the `theme` document; city and lat/long sit inside its DPAPI-encrypted place payload) is per device and not synced.
- **The phone's theme and sunset place (ADR 49).** On the phone, the theme name, light/dark mode (System, Light, Dark, At a set time, After sunset), colour mode and set times live in the `ui` preferences. They are per device and never synced, and there is no "match this phone" setting for the PC or watch. The place for After sunset is only ever typed (latitude/longitude) or picked from the bundled GeoNames list (`assets/cities.tsv`): the phone asks for no location permission. It is sealed with `TokenVault` (its own Keystore key) in the `ui` preferences as `place`, never synced, logged or sent, left out of backup files, and "Forget" in Settings → Appearance clears it. The switch uses one timer to the next flip moment (looked at again at least every 3 hours, and when the app comes back to the front), never polling.
- **The phone's Today layout (phone redesign, step 10).** Settings › Appearance › Today layout lets the person order Today's cards and put each above the fold, in "More today" or hidden. The choice is app-owned local state in the `ui` preferences (`todayOrder`, `todayPlacements`; `UiPrefs.todayLayout`), per device, never synced, in the settings backup's Look group, and nothing in it is a guard or a lock, so it is not locked during a session. It only changes what is drawn: what a session says (header, timer, wait, task list) is not in the layout and always comes first, and the guard banner, the lunch chip, "Link a PC", "Need it for work", "Not working today" and the import card can never be hidden (the first three never folded either; the Budget pace rides with the lunch chip, so hiding the focus ring never hides it; a stored value that would hide one counts as its default place, `TodayItem.allowed`). Nothing chosen draws Today exactly as before; a card added in a later version goes to the end in its default place. **Round 2 (R2b):** the editor is the Today layout sheet (Settings › Appearance › Today layout, and Today's ⋮ › Today layout) with two layouts, In a session and Not in a session, each part Shown / More / Hidden and one drag ≡ order; the in-session one is stored only once changed (`todaySessionOwn`, `todaySessionOrder`, `todaySessionPlacements` in the same `ui` preferences; until then a session uses the other layout, as before), with the same `TodayItem.allowed` clamp. On a phone, More (the old fold) is a row of More today chips, each opening a half-height sheet with the part; a phone with no plan yet still shows the import card on Today itself. The noise player is a bar in Today's thumb zone when its part is Shown and a sound folder is set (a More chip opens the Noise picker; Hidden hides both). The phone's Session sheet (Today's ⋯) sends only what ADR 42 already lets the phone send (Step away / I'm back, and End session as the end-early request, which still runs the PC's wait and reason); Pause timer, Add 10 minutes and End and mark the rest unfinished are greyed "Coming soon" for a PC session until ADR 82 is accepted.
- **The phone's Stats tab (phone redesign, Part 5).** The views are worked out on the phone from what it already holds: the sessions, drift (times only: no site or app name is shown) and daily goal in the sealed sync files received from the user's devices (removed devices' kept copies included, as the PC's totals count them; about 14 days from a newly linked device, kept up to 90), plus the phone's own data, which is read on the phone, never synced and never sent: sleep from Health Connect with the smart bedtime's nights, and time on the phone, pickups and the time inside counted focus from Android's usage records (Usage access). Nothing new is added to any sync file. The only thing stored is the tab's own choice in the `stats` preferences: the view last shown, the kind of range (Day, Week, Month, Year, Last7, Last30, Last90 or Custom; an older saved value still loads), and the order and hidden views of the chips (per device, never synced, not part of the settings backup). The position stepped to with the arrows and the custom dates are never saved: a restart opens on the current period, and a new day moves the current period on. The old Sleep and Phone cards of Today now live in Stats' Sleep and Phone views with the same content and the same permissions. A view with no data says so; it never draws zeros that were not measured. Known limit of the sync file: a session that is still running counts its away time until it ends (the file cuts only the planning stretch of a running session), so today's focus on the phone can be higher than on the PC until then.
- Core (`src/AccountabiliBuddy.Core`) has **no UI or OS dependencies**. Anything Windows-specific goes in `src/AccountabiliBuddy.Platform.Windows` behind a `Core/Ports` interface (ADR 4).

- History icons: app icons are read from the program file of a running app; site icons come only from the browser extension (the browser's own favicon cache, Chromium `favicon` permission / Firefox data: URLs). Both are cached as files in the data folder's `icons\`. Never download an icon.
- Detection cascade, cheapest first: process/title/URL rules → OCR only when still unknown. Event-driven, plus a slow periodic recheck. Keep CPU and battery use low.
- **Keyboard hook for hold-to-peek (ADR 53).** The one low-level keyboard hook (`Win32CtrlAltWatcher`) is installed only while hold-to-peek is on and the overlay is not turned off, and either the tile is on screen or Ctrl+Alt+H keeps a tile hidden that would otherwise show (so a held Ctrl+Alt can bring it up for a peek). "Would otherwise show" means exactly where a tile could: it is not installed while hidden over a full-screen app (when the corner clock or hide-in-full-screen is on), on the slim line, where the placement is hidden, in a call the app rules hide, or with nothing to show. It is removed when the overlay is turned off. It is reinstalled at each session start and end. It reads only whether Ctrl and Alt are down (also while the overlay is hidden); it never stores, logs, syncs or forwards a key, always passes every key on, and nothing may throw out of its callback. The panel's letter shortcuts are registered hotkeys, only while the panel is open: a peek takes none while peek keys are locked (the default) and only the safe ones (T, Up, Down, and H only when the Ctrl+Alt+H shortcut below is off) when unlocked; the sticky panel (Ctrl+Alt+Space) has them all (H likewise). Ctrl+Alt+H is never registered twice: while its shortcut is on, the panel does not register H (the shortcut's own registration serves, and the panel's H key does the same toggle); with the shortcut off, H is the panel's short Hide again. Reading any other key through it needs a new ADR.
- **Hiding and turning off the overlay (Ctrl+Alt+H, the panel's Disable).** Both live in the device-local `overlay` document (`OverlaySettings`: `HideShortcutOn`, `HiddenByShortcut`, `HideShortcutSeen`, `OverlayOn`, `OffFromPanel`); never synced, never in a profile, display only, so nothing here loosens anything and nothing waits. *Ctrl+Alt+H* is registered system-wide whenever the app runs, the `Hide shortcut` switch is on (default) and the overlay is not turned off; a taken shortcut shows its error on Settings › Alerts & on-screen › PC overlay (like Ctrl+Alt+Space). It toggles `HiddenByShortcut`: hidden until pressed again, across sessions and restarts. Hidden hides everything (tile, timeline, corner clock, slim line); a held Ctrl+Alt (with hold-to-peek on) shows the tile only once it has been held for "Peek opens after" (the panel opens with it), so another app's Ctrl+Alt+key shortcut does not flash it; an open sticky panel (Ctrl+Alt+Space or a tile click, also one a peek was promoted to) keeps the tile up until the panel closes; the time-up or alarm glow shows the tile for its 1.4 seconds (not where no tile could show, such as over a full-screen app); chimes always play. Reminder cards that rise from the tile fall back to the monitor's bottom right when no tile shows. Today shows a quiet line "Overlay hidden · Ctrl+Alt+H to show" with a Show button; the tray's "Show on-screen window again" and the Session menu's "Show on-screen overlay" also bring it back; switching the shortcut off shows it again. Hiding is allowed during a focus session (like the per-session "Hide on-screen timer"); the panel's Hide button stays the short hide (`QuickHideSeconds`). *Disable* (a footer button of the Ctrl+Alt panel, no confirm: the panel has no confirm pattern and a peek closes when the keys are let go) and the Settings switch "Show the on-screen overlay" set `OverlayOn` to false: no tile, no peek, no hook, no Ctrl+Alt+H, no Ctrl+Alt+Space; the chimes still play. - **Time with the PC off (ADR 60).** Any stretch with the PC off, asleep or AB closed during a session is non-work session time, never focus: an away stretch of kind `DeviceOff` built from the Interrupted and Resumed events, out of every total, and the session reaches other devices in pieces around it. - **Removing a device (ADR 54, 55).** Each device makes one ECDH P-256 key pair once and keeps it when it leaves a group: the private key DPAPI-encrypted in "sync.identity" on the PC, sealed by `TokenVault` (Keystore) in the phone's identity file; only the public key leaves the device, as the device card's optional `publicKey` (older apps ignore it). Any linked device can remove others, never during a focus session the user started (the PC's session lock; on the phone a PC session it tracks, `PlacementSession`, which also locks Leave group, joining another group, "continue unsynced" and the keep/erase choice). Removing writes a rekey file (`ABR1`) to every method in use (folder, Drive, relay), named by the first 6 bytes of SHA-256(old group id + maker id + "rekey") + `.absync`, sealed with the old group key: the epoch, the maker's id and name, the removed ids and names, and for each staying device with a public key (the maker included) the new link code wrapped by ECDH with a one-time key, HKDF and AES-256-GCM bound to that device's id and the old group id. A device applies only a rekey of the group it is in now, waits 3 s and takes the one from the lowest maker id (ids compared as unsigned RFC 4122 bytes) when two meet; a maker that finds another maker's rekey first applies it and asks the user to try again. A staying device switches to the new key in place and keeps everything it received; it deletes its own and the known devices' old-group files (never a rekey file). A device named as removed, or left without a copy, drops the key and is Unlinked: nothing syncs or is shared until the user picks add this device again (a normal join, same device id), continue unsynced (leaves the group, keeps its own and the received data) or start from scratch. Staying devices that can't get a copy (no public key, or only seen in others' `Seen` maps) are named to the remover beforehand; they are cut off and must link again. The removed list is local only (PC "sync.removed", phone `sync-removed.json`: ids, names, removed-at from the rekey's epoch, the keep/erase choice, the Unlinked state; everything live reads `ReceivedDevicesService.Active` / the phone's `received` flow, which leave removed devices out, while History, Stats' totals and the day bar read all of them); files from those ids are refused and the phone never forwards them (it does forward rekey files), until one arrives sealed with the new key and written after the removal. Each removed device carries the remover's keep/erase choice inside the rekey file (one byte after its name; any other value makes the file unreadable): every staying device that applies it records the choice and, for erase, forgets that device's received data (a PC or phone waits while a focus session the user started runs and does it afterwards: `ApplyPendingErasesAsync`, `applyPendingErases`); the maker erases its own copy after its backup is made or given up; a device that is itself removed keeps everything it has; shared list items stay. Restoring a backup of an erased device turns its choice to keep. Known limits: a removed device still holds the old key, so until the others apply the real rekey it could delete it or put a forged one in its place (needs a custom client; deleting it gives the same exposure); two removals whose files haven't reached each other's locations can still split the group; data the removed device could already read stays readable to it.

Keys the app holds for Cold Turkey are in the database and go with it. The first-run assistant then opens at its first page.

A backup is the device's last received sync file with the same fields left out as on the PC, as an `.absync`: to Google Drive as `AccountabiliBuddy sync/Backups/<name> (<4 hex of its id>)/<yyyy-MM-dd>.absync` (the name trimmed with no character filtered, "device" when blank, exactly the PC's folder rule; only when Drive is a sync method in use; `DriveFiles.uploadBackup`/`listBackups` under `drive.file`, the same-day file replaced; a 403 or any Drive failure offers the file), or to a file the user picks with the system file picker (`ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT`, written through the content resolver; the empty file the picker makes is deleted when no backup is written into it; nothing is written anywhere else). **Unlinked** (`sync.unlinked`, at app start and whenever a rekey unlinks the phone) is a full-screen state: no tabs, no header, no syncing, and Back only puts the app away; "by <name> on <MMM d, h:mm a>" is 12-hour; three equal choices with no default: Add this phone again (the existing join, scan the other device's QR with the camera or type the code, same device id; refused during a focus session, as on the PC), Continue unsynced here (`continueUnsynced`, refused during a focus session, then a one-time note "You can link again from Devices" and Today), Start from scratch (refused during a focus session; typed "start over"; the dialog says a private local copy is kept first, below). The Unlinked screen reads the focus-session lock again every 15 s, so its buttons come back when the lock lifts; Back on its two sub-pages returns to the choices. **Start from scratch scope** (`StartOver`): a copy of `files/` and `shared_prefs/` is made into `no_backup/start-over/<time>/` (app-private, never sent anywhere, the three newest kept, removed with the app; it holds the sealed identity, so it is as private as the app's own files), the watch is sent `UNLINK` (waits at most 6 s), WorkManager's work is cancelled (its own database lives in `no_backup/` and survives the clearing), a marker (`start-over.pending` with `pending <time>`) is written to `noBackupFilesDir`, and the app restarts in a new process; `Application.attachBaseContext` (before any content provider, store or loop opens a file) runs `StartOver.runPending`, which, for a `pending` marker younger than 10 minutes (an older one is deleted, nothing is cleared, and the next screen says so, so the user can ask again), first rewrites the marker to `clearing` (a `clearing` marker is always finished, whatever its age, so a cut-off wipe never leaves a half-cleared app), then releases the folder permissions the user granted and deletes the contents of `files/`, `cache/`, `code_cache/`, `shared_prefs/`, `databases/`, `datastore/` and the app's external files and cache folders, then the marker. AlarmManager alarms set before survive the process, but the restarted app's start-up re-derives timers, bedtime reminders and blocks from the empty stores, so none fires into old state. That removes the identity (a new device id), the group code, the past keys, the removed list, received devices, every store and preference of the app; the Android Keystore keys stay (the new identity seals with them again), as do Android's permission grants, the Google Drive grant held by Play services, Curbox, the other apps and the user's other devices. **Restore on the phone** (Devices tab › "Restore from an older device…"): lists the Drive `Backups/` files newest first and takes a file from `ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT`; a backup opens with the group's current key or the past key of the group in its header (`BackupOpener`, ADR 56), with the PC's refusals (not in a group, not a backup or over 16 MB, a group no key is kept for, this phone's own data); restoring brings the device in as a received device under its old name with no sealed bytes (never carried on) and records it as removed (`SyncCoordinator.restoreBackup`; a device linked again with newer data is left alone; an erase choice turns to keep). **The watch** (`WatchPaths.PHONE_STATE` "/phone-state", one byte, 1 = this phone is unlinked, 0 = fine; a flag only, nothing private; sent when it changes and with every "/hello" answer) shows "Your phone was removed from your synced devices. Choose what to do on your phone." as its first page and keeps its snapshot until the phone links again or continues unsynced (the watch link itself is not changed by "continue unsynced"). `UNLINK` (the phone unlinked the watch, or started from scratch) no longer wipes at once: the watch asks "Forget data" / "Keep for now" (state in the watch's `phoneNotice` preferences; Keep silences further `UNLINK`s until a linked snapshot arrives; either choice also clears the "phone unlinked" page, since a phone that no longer has this watch linked never sends the 0).
- **Scanning a link code on the phone (ADR 57).** Devices & sync ("Link this phone") and the screen for adding an unlinked phone again have **Scan** and **Paste** next to the typed code. Scan opens an in-app camera screen (CameraX preview and analysis, ZXing core, QR only); the `CAMERA` permission is asked only when Scan is tapped, and `uses-feature android.hardware.camera` is `required=false`, so a phone without a camera installs and can paste. Frames are decoded in memory and dropped: nothing is saved (no photo, no file, no document), nothing is sent, no connection is opened, and the code is never logged. No ML Kit or Play Services. Paste reads the clipboard only when tapped. Both hand the text to the existing join path (`JoinLink.parse` then the "Join <name>'s devices?" confirmation, or `SyncKeyCode.parse` for a bare code, which only fills the field); there are no extra parsing rules. A QR that isn't a join link or code is reported and scanning continues.

The light stays hidden while the phone is linked to nothing, as before. The Settings search is a fixed list in the app (`SettingsSearch`: terms, pages and rows) worked out in memory while the user types: the typed text is never stored, logged, sent or kept in a history (it is dropped when the user leaves Settings), a result only opens a page at a row (`settings/<page>#<row>`) and changes nothing. The remove-device and restore sheets on the phone draw the same pages in the same order as before (ADR 54, 55, 56): the lock is read again on every page and on confirm, the sheet cannot be swiped, tapped or backed away while the removal is working, and the backup paths are unchanged.
- **The phone's sharing switches rest during a focus session (Part 6a fix round 1; the PC's rule: its Devices page refuses them too).** While the phone tracks a session the user started (`SyncCoordinator.removalLockedReason`, the same check as Leave group, removing a device and joining another group), `SyncCoordinator.saveSettings` refuses, and returns the reason for, any change that takes something away from how this phone shares (`PhoneSyncSettings.narrowedBy`): QR codes, Sync files, Shared folder, Google Drive or Own relay going on → off, or one that stays on being pointed elsewhere (another folder; another relay address, pin or admission key). That covers the switches, Choose folder, the relay's "Use this address, pin and key", and a same-group join link that carries a relay. Turning a way on, Sign in again, Sync now, the folder's minutes and the wake-up alarm fields stay allowed, so a session that froze with no end can still be recovered. The rows are disabled with the reason as visible text; the gate is in the coordinator, so a stale screen is refused too. Without it, turning Drive off froze the phone on the last PC file, and Essentials and Phone down stopped after the 30-minute no-end limit while the PC session ran.
- **Turning off the phone's last way of syncing waits 10 minutes, and nothing shortens it (Part 6a fix rounds 2 and 3; a looser change, like the sleep and placement waits).** The ways that bring the PC's file by themselves are Shared folder, Google Drive and Own relay (`SyncMethod`); QR codes and Sync files only work when the user scans or opens something, so they do not count and apply at once. A way *counts* (`SyncOffWait.counted`) only when it has brought a file from another device of the group *and* has been on, pointing at the same folder or relay, for 10 minutes (the "on since" times and "has delivered" marks are in prefs `syncOff`, never synced, the relay's key hashed). "Delivered" is recorded in `SyncCoordinator.acceptSealed` for the destination the file came from and is dropped when the way is pointed elsewhere; a copy of a file this phone wrote to that place itself to carry it on (`SyncOffWait.noteCarried`) is not a delivery, so a dummy way (an empty folder the phone carries files into, an address that goes nowhere) never counts and gives no way round the wait, while a real folder that delivers keeps its protection. What is on when the app first gets this rule counts for time at once; Drive and the relay count as delivering only if the phone holds a received file that came by them. The shared folder is never migrated (a held file's method has no destination and survives a re-point, so it could mark a wrongly picked folder): a real folder earns its mark from the pass at start-up. The migration also records every held file as carried by each way other than the one it came by (the old version wrote those copies there itself), so a dummy folder holding carried copies earns nothing. The folder's "only this phone's file" warning stops once the folder has delivered. A way that does not count protects nothing: between sessions it can be pointed elsewhere (a wrongly picked folder, for example the phone's own sub-folder) or turned off at once; during a focus session the user started nothing changes (the rule above). Between sessions `SyncCoordinator.saveSettings` keeps the last way that counts on when asked to turn it off, starts `SyncOffWait` (prefs `syncOff`, survives a restart, no "apply now") and its row says "Turns off at 4:05 PM" with Cancel (any time). Turning another way on, or a way that doesn't count off, does not shorten or skip it. The Shared folder row always shows the switch and "Choose folder", enabled or disabled with the reason as text under them, and, after a pass that found only this phone's file in the folder (a count of files, nothing read), says so and where the other devices' files should be. Pointing the last way that counts at another folder or relay (Choose folder, "Use this address...", a same-group join link) is refused with a reason unless that way reports a problem, so a broken one can still be fixed. A settings import (`PhoneBackup.import`) keeps this phone's sync settings when the file would leave no way that counts on and unchanged (the import dialog says so). The turn-off applies only while no session runs (`removalLockedReason`): when the wait is due the app syncs once first (`CompanionApp.watchSyncOff`), so a session that started during the wait is seen before the way goes; a wait that ends in a session applies after it. Turning off one of several ways that count, and turning a way on, apply at once. During a session turning a way off is refused outright (the rule above). Leave group and joining another group are not held by this wait (they already cost a re-link).
- **Settings import rests during a focus session (Part 6a fix round 1).** `PhoneBackup.import` takes `removalLockedReason` as its first check and returns it, so no screen can get around it (a backup file can carry a paused guard, a wide-open Essentials, a switched-off sync); App & data › Backup › "Import…" is disabled with the reason as visible text and the lock is read again at confirm. Exporting and auto-backup are not affected.
- **Voice for "Park a thought" on the phone (Part 2).** The pill above the phone's tab bar (Today only since Part 3: Tasks has its own add field there) can take the thought by voice: its microphone starts the phone's own speech screen (`RecognizerIntent.ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH`, offline preferred) and puts the text it hands back into the text box, where the user reads it and parks it (it never parks by itself; a cancelled screen does nothing). AccountabiliBuddy receives only that text: it holds no `RECORD_AUDIO` permission, never touches audio and opens no connection for it; the voice service is the system's and may itself be online, outside AccountabiliBuddy's connections (the box says so). The microphone is not drawn when the phone has no such service (a `<queries>` entry lets the app ask). Parked text goes into the shared parked list as before: Keystore-sealed, never logged; next to a PC older than the shared lists it goes to that PC as a park action through the sealed sync files, as Come back to does. Typed text never goes through the voice service and stays on the user's own devices (the box says so).
- **A stepped-away session stays a session on the phone (Part 2 fix round 2).** While a PC session is stepped away with the countdown paused (a Step away or side task with the Pause timer setting, a lunch, Pause timer) the PC pauses the countdown and moves the phase's start and end forward by the time away only when the user is back (`SessionEngine`, `SessionState.CountdownTime`) (a Step away or side task with the timer running, ADR 77, goes out with no `awaySince`: the phone counts down to the real end and doesn't show the PC as stepped away until the Android run carries it); its sync file therefore keeps the old end and says `awaySince`. The phone reads the end the PC will set on return (`SyncRunning.phaseEndAt(now)`: the end plus the time away so far), so a step away that crosses the old end never ends the session for the phone: the guard settings lock, the removal and sleep-off locks, a pending "turn off", Essentials, Phone down, placement, Curbox hard mode (which starts its focus group again for what is left), Today and the watch snapshot all keep holding. A paused session has no end to run out, so stepping away never releases the phone earlier than not stepping away would: the hold lasts until the old end or until the PC's file is 30 minutes old (the limit of a session with no set end, ADR 45), whichever is later, and never past the 12-hour file limit (`SyncRunning.isAwayOverdue`: stepped away, old end passed and file over 30 minutes old). The PC rewrites its file every sync interval (at most 15 minutes) while it is on and syncing (folder timer 1/5/15 minutes, Drive and relay on their polls; none with QR or file sync only, where the phone holds until the old end), so a PC that really went away stops holding the phone and it can never stay locked for good; the file's age includes delivery, so a healthy PC's file can look old for a moment past the old end until the next one arrives. The guard banner says "Settings rest until your session ends" while stepped away. Curbox's focus group starts again for the time left after the minutes it asked for run out, but not during lunch or a break. `OtherPcSessions` on the PC is not changed (its only use is the lunch's "another PC is in a session"); the watch compares the moved end from the phone's last push.
- **Which PC the phone's Today shows (Part 2 fix round 1, amends ADR 42).** Today follows the PC that has a live session (`PcFileControl.liveRunning`): the controlled PC (the one picked in Devices, else the newest file) while it runs one, else another linked PC that does. Step away, I'm back and End early go to that PC and its own wait and reason still decide when it ends (the phone only names the target; a named PC whose file is gone is refused, never swapped for another). Start shows only when no linked PC runs a session and always goes to the controlled PC.
- **The phone's Tasks tab edits the shared lists, Later included (Part 3, ADR 48, 50).** The phone adds, renames, ticks, reorders, moves and deletes the next session's tasks, the come-backs and the **Later list** (title, steps, estimate chips 25/50/90 or a number, note, category, due date, delete with Undo, Add to Today, move to Come back to), with the PC's model and merge, also while a PC session runs (those lists are not the PC's checklist): every edit stamps only the item it touches with `SharedListMerge.nextStamp` (the newest edit wins per item, a delete is a tombstone with no text kept 30 days, items other devices have are carried on as they came, fields the phone has no screen for, like a planned day part, are copied unchanged), and `laterForSync` always writes the list, even empty. A task's **note** is private text: kept Keystore-sealed with the lists (`SharedListsStore`), sent only inside the sealed sync files to the user's own devices (and, with a Later item added to a running PC session, inside the sealed `taskAdd` action, whose pending copy `ActionStore` also keeps Keystore-sealed), never logged, never in a notification, never on the watch. **Ticking a Later item is a shared edit, not a delete** (user decision, additive wire change): the item keeps its text and gets an optional `doneAt` in the sealed sync files (same stamps, newest edit wins; an app that predates the field ignores it, passes the item on without it and shows it open; the field joins the merge tie-break only when present). Either device's tick sets it, either device's Undo or Put back clears it with a newer stamp, and a done item shows in the phone's Done (today's, and older ones with their day) and in the PC's Done today and Archive. The PC writes its "done outside a session" record once per tick, under the Later item's id: its own tick writes it, a `doneAt` that arrives from another device writes it at that merge only (a restart or a merge that brings nothing new does not), and a reopened item removes it; the record is never focus time or a session. A done Later item ages out after 90 days (the PC's record retention) into an ordinary tombstone with no text. The **search** on the Tasks tab (titles, steps, notes) and the category chip only filter what is already in memory: the text is never stored, saved in the instance state, logged or sent. During a running session This session is the PC's checklist and add, edit, reorder and tick go to the PC as actions it applies under its own rules (three tasks, a 4th parked; nothing here ends a session or lifts a guard). **Add to Today** from Later or Come back to during a session is a `taskAdd` and the source leaves its list only when the PC really added the task: a refusal leaves it, a PC at its limit parks it as a come-back (answer status `parked`, `ok` with a message from older PCs; a come-back carries only title, category and a step count) and then a Later item stays with its note, steps, estimate and due date, and so does one edited here after it was sent. The pending hold (ids and a time, no text) survives a restart and a second send of the same item is refused while it waits. A done next-session task of an earlier day still counts toward the 3 (as on the PC) and is listed under Done so it can be put back or deleted. Next to a PC older than the shared lists the Tasks tab says so and shows that PC's own come-backs read-only (tick only, parks sent to it shown as sending); an edit is never shown as done when it went nowhere. "Put in plan" is not on the phone.
- Install and data safety (ADR 10): packaged with Velopack into `%LocalAppData%\AccountabiliBuddyApp` (never the data folder's name); updates come from locally built packages or, in the installed app, the public releases repo (ADR 12: Settings → Updates; asks before installing unless automatic updates are on; never installs during a session; dev builds never check). ## UI

### Stable layout (round 2; word for word from the design pack's change note §1)

1. **Fixed anchors.** The title bar, nav rail (PC) or bottom nav (phone), page title and the page's first content row sit at the same position on every tab. Switching tabs never moves them.
2. **Nothing pushes.** Anything that appears, opens or grows slides over the content or uses space that was already reserved. This covers the Today sidebar, the Shelf, the title-bar session control on hover, banners and toasts. Lists keep their width and position.
   - **When something has to move, animate it.** If a layout change can't be avoided, it eases into place (about 150–250 ms, ease-out) instead of jumping, so the eye can follow where things went. No instant snaps. Respect the system's reduce-motion setting by shortening the motion to a quick fade.
   - **Exception: the Tasks Shelf.** It stays a deliberate two-pane split that narrows the list, as in the Tasks spec, but it opens and closes with the eased motion above.
3. **Clicking never moves the thing you clicked.** No reflow under the pointer. This holds when a row's state changes (tick, select, expand) and when a section count changes.
4. **Pop-ups open next to what opened them.** Menus, sliders and pickers are anchored to their trigger, centred on it when they fit. Near a window edge they align to that edge instead of overflowing (for example, the session ⋯ menu at the right of the title bar). Full-screen or centred dialogs are only for confirmations and Can't-miss reminders.
5. **Controls stay next to what they act on.** Extra width goes to content (task text, steps), never to the space between a value and its buttons.
6. **One place per thing.** The same item is never shown twice in a page's content (for example, the current task is not repeated next to the timer). App chrome is exempt: the title-bar session timer stays on every tab, including Today, because it is a fixed anchor (rule 1).
7. **Session start or end keeps the layout.** Today's in-session and not-in-session layouts share the same skeleton: the session or start card in the same slot, and the task list in the same slot.

### Title bar appearance toggle (round 2, ADR 49)

- **The title bar's light/dark toggle and theme menu** (PC; click flips light/dark, right-click or a 450 ms hold opens Mode, Theme and Theme settings…) write only this PC's `theme` document through `AppTheme`, the same as Settings › Appearance: per device, never synced, never in a profile. Appearance is not a guard setting, so the toggle works during a focus session; "Theme settings…" opens Settings, where Settings' own session locks apply.

### The PC's Today layout (round 2 R2)

- **Settings › Today** (two layouts, in a session and not; each Today sidebar part Panel / Chip / Off with drag ≡ or Alt+↑/↓ to reorder; the side column during and outside a session; Show finished today) is app-owned local state in the `today.layout` document (`TodayLayoutService`), per device, never synced, a device setting in backups restored through the service, the same in both modes. It only changes what Today draws, so it is not locked during a session (like Appearance and the phone's Today layout) and changes apply at once. The session card (or start card) and This session are not parts of it and always show, in the same slots in and out of a session (rule 7). Outside ProductaBuddy the lunch part can never be off: Settings offers only Panel and Chip, and a stored Off (from ProductaBuddy or a backup) draws it as a chip (`TodayLayoutRules.Effective`). The PC has no guard banner on Today; its notices (waits, overlay off) are not parts and always show. The sidebar's open / hidden state is memory only (until the app restarts); a hidden sidebar is out of the Tab order and not drawn. Outside ProductaBuddy the lunch chip also stays in view while the sidebar is hidden to its rail (a session's default): the rail carries it whenever today has a lunch to show (`TodayLayoutRules.LunchOnRail`). A mode switch redraws the sidebar and Settings › Today at once (`TodayLayoutService.ModeChanged`), so the lunch rule follows the mode in force. The title bar's Session menu and the session card's ⋯ end a session only through `SessionEndFlow` (the same wait and reason as before).

### The PC's Tasks layout (round 2 R3)

- **The Tasks page is planning only.** Today › This session, Queued, Reminders due today and Finished today are on the Today tab; Tasks shows a Today strip with their counts (no timer). A Shelf task dropped on the strip, or its row's ← (button or key), moves to Today › Queued as an ordinary task-list move with Undo (`TodayStripDrop`); the strip never puts a task in This session, so the session's action cap is never in play there. Into This session stays the cap's own paths (Move ›, the swap dialog, a drop on This session where it is shown).
- **Column order** (Group by Bins, Label or Tag; None is one list) is this PC's own: `TaskLayoutPreferences.ColumnOrder` in the `tasks.layout` document, per grouping, never synced, never in a view or a sync file, a device setting in backups. Syncing it with the phone's page order waits for ADR 83 (Proposed).
- **During a session** (Settings › Tasks › During a session › Hide the Shelf, as the session layout started with it): the Shelf's columns hide behind "Show the Shelf" (Ctrl+\) with a quiet count; adding, searching (a search shows what it finds) and the strip still work. Shown, it is at full strength, and it hides again when the user leaves the Tasks tab or holds Reset. It only changes what the page draws, so it is not locked and loosens nothing.
